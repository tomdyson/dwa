Build your own little apps

When I build simple web apps, I no longer write code - I describe what I want to an AI coding assistant, which I've already taught how to make apps and host them for free on a platform called Cloudflare.

This guide gets you set up to do the same. By the end you'll have your own app machine: say "make me a page where friends can pick dates for dinner" and a few minutes later you'll have a private web page, with a password, where people tick boxes and leave notes. Or say "make a sign-up page for my Tuesday classes, twelve places each" and you'll have a booking page anyone can use. Answers and bookings will save as they go, and you can ask Claude what everyone said or who's coming.

This page is itself one of those apps. Tick things off as you go, and if you get stuck, write in the box at the end of each step. I'll see what you write. Your ticks and notes are private: only you and I can see them. They're tied to this browser, so if you'd like to carry on from another computer, ask me for a link that brings them with you.

How it fits together

Claude does the technical work: it writes the code, runs the commands and fixes its own mistakes. You stay in charge: you say what you want, approve what it does, and check the result. You won't need to type anything into the Terminal yourself.

Cloudflare runs your apps on the internet. Its free plan is far more than you'll need, and you won't need to give it a card.

Allow about two hours for steps 1 to 7, with a cup of tea. Steps 5 and 6 are mostly waiting while Claude works.

First, name your app machine

It's the word you'll use to ask Claude for a new app, and it's the first part of your apps' web address to begin with (.yourname.workers.dev). The yourname part is a different name, which you'll pick in step 6. If you later move to your own domain (step 9), you can pick a different address. Mine is called dwa, for "disposable web apps", but my apps live at with.tomd.org. Keep it short, lowercase, no spaces: pages, asks, your initials. The instructions below fill in whatever you choose.

When things go wrong

  • Errors are normal. Claude hits them constantly and usually fixes them itself. If it seems stuck, copy the error (or take a screenshot and drag it in) and say "this happened, what does it mean and what should we do?"
  • Ask it to explain. "Explain that like I've never done this before" always works. So does "why are you asking me that?"
  • If it's going round in circles, press the stop button and say "Stop. Tell me in plain English what you're trying to do and what's going wrong."
  • A long conversation gets muddled. Start a fresh one and say "Read the README in this folder and tell me where we'd got to."
  • If in doubt about a permission request, say no and ask what it was for. Nothing here should ever need to touch files outside your Apps folder, apart from its own settings.
  • Or write in the box under the step you're on and I'll have a look.
